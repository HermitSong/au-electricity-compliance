# Enterprise Pilot Gate

Status: preparation only. No real enterprise pilot or cost saving has been measured.

## Objective

Reduce total human time per correctly resolved Australian electricity compliance
matter without increasing customer harm, unsafe clearance or missed obligations.
English is the supported knowledge, query and answer workflow. No translation
feature is required for this release.

## Required Before Real Records

- Record the legal entity, licences, regulated roles, jurisdictions and actual
  assets/customer classes. Do not equate a group brand with every licensed entity.
- Approve access, processing purpose, model-provider disclosure and retention for
  the specific records. Local paths and declared reviewer names are not access
  control, encryption, authentication or proof of authority.
- Keep corporate facts, transcripts, policies, model requests, outputs and review
  decisions in a controlled private store outside the public KB and GitHub tree.
- Name an accountable operating owner and an escalation contact. A model may
  prepare an answer; material action requires the applicable enterprise approval.
- For time-sensitive matters, establish the actual trigger, awareness time,
  applicable calendar, timezone and source rule before calculating a deadline.
- Freeze acceptance criteria and an unseen evaluation sample before running the
  pilot. Synthetic examples and software test counts do not satisfy this gate.

## Initial Workflows

1. A proposed NEM battery registration, bidding or settlement decision with its
   approved technical configuration and application scope supplied explicitly.
2. A retail billing-review or complaint scenario with the licensed entity,
   jurisdiction, customer class, actual communications and timestamps supplied.
3. Candidate screening of an authorised operating record, followed by fact
   confirmation and a rule/case evidence brief. No-match is never clearance.

These are evaluation cohorts, not claims that all three workflows are presently
cleared for self-service. Select questions supported by reviewed operative sources;
record blocked or partial answers instead of silently removing them from results.

## Matter Record

Retain the original question and source facts, material assumptions, answer date,
source and clause versions, historical case status, required subquestions, draft,
independent review, counter-search, unresolved facts, proposed controls, accountable
owner, approved actions and actual closure evidence. A responsible person confirms
fact assertions and any legal clock. Do not populate these fields merely to make a
record appear complete.

## Measurements

| Metric | Measurement boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Complete correct resolution | All material requested issues resolved under the frozen rubric; not just correct statements among answers attempted |
| Material-error and unsafe-clearance rate | Include mistaken permission, wrong dates, missed exceptions and inappropriate jurisdiction transfer |
| Case use | Cite relevant known real cases; absence of a citation alone is not proof of a false substantive answer |
| Human time | Intake, research, review, correction, escalation and maintenance minutes per correctly resolved matter |
| Total cost | Human effort plus model, search, storage and maintenance costs; use observed costs, not token count as a proxy |
| Coverage | Include unanswered, partial, excluded and out-of-scope matters in separate denominators |
| Screening quality | False alarms and missed material issues by category; assess lexical-only and AI-assisted modes separately |

Compare against the existing enterprise process on a matched sample reviewed by a
qualified independent domain reviewer. Report the sample, dates, exclusions and
uncertainty. Do not claim universal accuracy, professional outperformance or cost
reduction from a zero-error small sample.

## Publication

GitHub examples must be synthetic and reproducible. Review source redistribution
rights, secrets, personal information, tracked history, archives and support logs.
The public release must separate functioning features, diagnostic results, pilot
prerequisites and unimplemented capabilities. No provider key or corporate sample
is supplied by the repository.
