## Repository Description

Source-available Australian electricity compliance research toolkit. Free for
enterprise internal use; paid external services and resale require permission.
Evidence-bound temporal case retrieval. Research preview, not legal advice.

## LinkedIn Draft

I am making Australian Electricity Compliance available for inspection and
enterprise internal use: https://github.com/HermitSong/au-electricity-compliance

The aim is to reduce repeated regulatory research and routine compliance
confirmation costs for Australian electricity businesses, without hiding missing
evidence, historical-rule conflicts or uncertainty.

The project connects authored knowledge, public case records, source references
and temporal review workflows. It includes a synthetic meeting-screening example
and a command-line answer/reviewer handoff, not an autonomous hosted legal service.

This is a source-available research preview, not unrestricted open source. Learning,
research and enterprise internal use are free under the project licence. Resale,
white-label offerings and paid external services require separate permission.
Official source materials retain their own rights and attribution.

Coverage is incomplete. The project is not legal advice, regulator-endorsed or
proven to outperform professionals. Tests measure their stated software scope,
not universal legal accuracy or demonstrated cost savings. The custom licence has
not received independent legal review.

Contributions with exact official sources, dates and subsequent case treatment
are welcome under the published terms. Never post real customer records, company
meetings or privileged advice to the repository.
