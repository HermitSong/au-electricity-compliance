# English-Only Accuracy Optimisation

Date: 13 September 2026. This release is a bounded implementation step toward
lower-cost, reliable enterprise electricity-compliance research. It is not a
nationwide-completeness, professional-outperformance or operational-clearance claim.

No Chinese answering, translation or bilingual optimisation was added. Authored

## Source and Rule Corrections

The operative-source review uses the [AEMC NER v254](https://energy-rules.aemc.gov.au/ner/818),
effective 4 September 2026, with exact selections from its official full PDF.
Each approval is limited to the stated clauses, exceptions, context and review date.
It is an independent AI source review, not human legal certification.

Important corrections include the inclusive 5 MW BDU test, common-connection
aggregation, all stated alternative classifications, registration versus exemption,
intermediary responsibilities, separate GEN/LOAD bands, and offer/dispatch/settlement
distinctions. The settlement selection includes NER 11.179's active transition;
the normal seven-/nine-business-day shorthand cannot determine a September payment
date without the relevant billing-period calendar and statement receipt.

FY27 fees are official administrative schedules, not legislation or total-project
quotes. The fixed fee, extra assessment effort, optional classifications and GST
basis are kept distinct. Earlier FY26 figures remain labelled historical.

The source reviewer found an initially omitted settlement transition and withheld
approval until it was included. Code review found incomplete-range admission,
reviewer identity/date issues, duplicate IDs, forged packet text, uncited duties
inside limitations, whitespace-only citations and misleading identity comparisons.
Findings and first failing tests are retained rather than replaced by later passes.

## Reflection and Next Work

The most useful improvements were correcting a real transition, admitting complete
source ranges, retrieving a known case and removing false blockers. Adding more
documents or more agent roles alone would not have fixed these defects.

Remaining priority work is concrete:

1. Bind the current AEMO BDU interface and validation procedure, including outward
   GEN/LOAD identification and effective-band/loss-factor rules. Q05 is incomplete
   and Q06's correct conditional reasoning still lacks that procedural source.
2. Review current FCAS-specific provisions, MASS and applicable plant evidence.
   The new case history and scoped dispatch binding do not complete Q12's current
   operating-obligation mapping.
3. Extend exact review to high-frequency retail billing, complaints, hardship,
   life-support and disconnection workflows. The legacy routes have not become
   approved merely because these six NEM routes were reviewed.
4. Validate natural-language intake and question decomposition, then evaluate a
   fresh held-out sample against the actual Desktop archive and configured providers.
   Keep development retests separate from unseen evaluation.
5. Implement protected maintainer writes and reviewer identity controls, measured
   source-refresh operations, and the private enterprise matter/approval process.
   Current hashes and declared reviewer IDs are not authenticated authorization.
6. Run the matched enterprise pilot and measure human time per correctly resolved
   matter, total cost, missed obligations, false alarms and unsafe clearance. No
   real cost saving or professional outperformance has yet been demonstrated.

Australia-wide public-original collection remains a parallel objective. The
coverage ledger and unresolved source families are unchanged; this release does
not establish all-Australia or twenty-year public-record completeness. Nothing was
published to GitHub or LinkedIn, and no corporate record was uploaded.
