# Disclaimer

This repository provides **regulatory information, not legal advice**. It was compiled with care and validated by a documented blind-exam process (see `review/`), but Australian energy law changes continuously and this material carries an as-of baseline of **August 2026**.

- Do not rely on this repository as the sole basis for licensing, investment, connection, safety, or enforcement-exposure decisions. Engage qualified Australian legal counsel and/or confirm directly with the relevant regulator (AEMC, AEMO, AER, CER, state bodies).
- Pages marked `draft-researched` were researched with citations but not independently re-verified against every official source.
- Claims marked ⚠️ are known uncertainties, deliberately flagged.
