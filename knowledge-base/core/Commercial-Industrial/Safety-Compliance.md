# Safety and Compliance -- Commercial-Industrial (100 kW - 5 MW)

## WHS Compliance

The Work Health and Safety framework applies to all C&I installations. The PCBU (Person Conducting a Business or Undertaking) has primary duty of care.

### Key WHS Requirements for C&I Solar/Battery

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Safe Work Method Statements (SWMS) | Required for high-risk construction work (working at heights >2m, electrical work) |
| Risk Assessment | Must be completed before installation commences |
| PPE | Appropriate PPE for electrical and rooftop work |
| Working at Heights | Fall protection systems; edge protection; harness systems per AS/NZS 1891 |
| Electrical Safety | Licensed electrical workers; isolation procedures |
| DC Safety | PV arrays energised during daylight; DC isolation per AS/NZS 5033 |
| Asbestos Management | Pre-installation asbestos survey for older commercial buildings |
| Emergency Procedures | Fire safety plan; isolation procedures clearly labelled |

## Electrical Safety Regulations by State

### State-Specific Requirements

| State | Regulator | Key Requirements |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| NSW | SafeWork NSW; Fair Trading (electrical licence) | Electrical installations must be inspected and certified by licensed electrician; Certificate of Compliance Electrical Work (CCEW) required |
| VIC | Energy Safe Victoria (ESV) | Certificate of Electrical Safety (CES) required within 24 hours of completion; prescribed installation testing |
| QLD | Electrical Safety Office | Electrical Safety Certificate required; Form 18 electrical work notice |
| SA | Office of the Technical Regulator (OTR) | Certificate of Compliance for prescribed electrical work |
| TAS | WorkSafe Tasmania; CBOS | Certificate of Compliance for electrical work |
| WA | Building and Energy (DEMIRS) | Electrical contractor licence required; Preliminary Notice and Electrician's Work Report |

### DC Isolator Requirements

DC isolators have been a significant safety focus following rooftop fire incidents:

| Requirement | Current Standard |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Rooftop DC Isolator | AS/NZS 5033:2021 removed the mandatory requirement for a rooftop DC isolator adjacent to the inverter for new installations (changed from 2014 edition) |
| Array DC Isolator | Still required at the PV array for maintenance isolation |
| String-level Isolation | Recommended for systems >30 kW for maintenance safety |
| Rapid Shutdown | Not mandatory in AS/NZS 5033:2021 but increasingly adopted for C&I rooftop systems (follows US NEC 690.12 trends) |
| Labelling | Clear labelling of all DC isolation points per AS/NZS 5033 |

### Battery-Specific Safety

| Requirement | Standard / Guideline |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Installation clearances | AS/NZS 5139; minimum distances from habitable spaces |
| Ventilation | Ventilation requirements for indoor BESS per AS/NZS 5139 |
| Fire suppression | Fire detection and suppression system for BESS >100 kWh |
| Emergency procedures | Battery-specific emergency response plan |
| Signage | Hazard signage per AS/NZS 5139 and state fire brigade requirements |
| CFA/MFB notification | VIC: notify Country Fire Authority or Metropolitan Fire Brigade of BESS installations |

## Building Compliance

### Building Code of Australia (BCA) / National Construction Code (NCC)

| Requirement | Application |
|-------------|-------------|
| Structural assessment | Roof structural adequacy for PV system loading (typically 15-25 kg/m2) |
| Wind loading | AS/NZS 1170.2 wind loading assessment for mounting system |
| Fire safety | PV and BESS must not compromise building fire safety features |
| Access | Maintenance access provisions; clearance from edges |
| Waterproofing | Roof penetration waterproofing for mounting systems |

### Development Approval

C&I systems typically require:

| Requirement | Threshold |
|-------------|-----------|
| Exempt development (no DA required) | Rooftop solar on industrial/commercial buildings in most states (subject to conditions) |
| Complying development | Ground-mount solar in industrial zones; minor structures |
| Development Application | Systems >1 MW; ground-mount in non-industrial zones; heritage areas |

## Ongoing Safety Obligations

| Obligation | Frequency |
|-----------|-----------|
| Electrical safety inspection | Per state regulation; typically every 5 years for commercial premises |
| Thermographic inspection | Recommended annually for PV systems >100 kW |
| Visual inspection | 6-monthly for connections, cables, mounting |
| Performance monitoring | Continuous via monitoring system |
| BESS health monitoring | Continuous; battery management system (BMS) monitoring |
