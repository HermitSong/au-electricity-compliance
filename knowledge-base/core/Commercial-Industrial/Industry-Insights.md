# Industry Insights -- Commercial-Industrial (100 kW - 5 MW)

## Corporate PPA Market Growth

### Market Overview

The Australian corporate PPA market has matured significantly, with C&I buyers increasingly driving new renewable investment.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Cumulative corporate PPAs signed (NEM) | >15 GW contracted since 2017 |
| Average PPA tenor | 10-12 years |
| Dominant buyer sectors | Mining, data centres, food & beverage, retail, manufacturing |
| Contract sizes | 50-650 MW (utility-scale); 1-50 MW (C&I direct) |
| Pricing trend | Sub-$40/MWh for large off-site PPAs; $50-80/MWh for on-site |

### PPA Structures in Use

| Structure | Description | C&I Suitability |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| On-site PPA | Developer owns rooftop/ground-mount system; C&I pays per kWh | Best for sites with high self-consumption and suitable roof/land |
| Off-site / Virtual PPA | Financial contract linked to a remote renewable project | Suitable for multi-site corporates; no physical generation on-site |
| Retail Green PPA | Renewable energy bundled with retail supply contract | Simplest; suitable for smaller C&I |
| Aggregated PPA | Multiple C&I buyers share output of a single large project | Enables mid-tier firms to access utility-scale economics |
| Sleeved PPA | Physical PPA intermediated through a retailer | Retailer manages settlement; C&I bears volume risk |

### Key Trends

1. **Aggregation** -- Mid-tier C&I firms (1-10 MW demand) increasingly pooling demand to access lower PPA rates through aggregation platforms
2. **Bankability shift** -- Lenders accepting corporate PPA revenue as bankable offtake for project finance
3. **Scope 2 reporting** -- Market-based Scope 2 accounting driving demand for LGC-bundled PPAs
4. **24/7 matching** -- Leading corporates moving beyond annual MWh matching toward hourly renewable matching (Google, Microsoft model)

## Behind-the-Meter + VPP

### Virtual Power Plant (VPP) Participation

C&I battery systems are increasingly enrolled in VPP programs to earn additional revenue.

| VPP Program | Operator | C&I Eligibility |
|-------------|----------|----------------|
| Tesla VPP | Tesla / Retailer | Systems with Tesla Powerwall/Megapack; aggregated dispatch |
| AGL VPP | AGL | C&I batteries enrolled via AGL retail agreement |
| Origin Loop | Origin Energy | C&I battery aggregation |
| AEMO VPP Demonstrations | [[../Regulatory-Bodies#AEMO|AEMO]] | Trial programs; select participants |
| ShineHub VPP | ShineHub | Aggregated residential and small C&I |

### VPP Revenue Streams

| Revenue | Mechanism | Typical Value |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Wholesale arbitrage | Charge during low prices; discharge during peaks | $20-60/kW/year |
| FCAS | Fast frequency response via aggregated dispatch | $10-30/kW/year |
| Network support | Reduce peak demand on constrained feeders | $5-20/kW/year |
| Capacity value | CIS or network capacity credit | Emerging |

### Behind-the-Meter Value Optimisation

```
Revenue Stack for C&I Battery:

1. Demand charge reduction      [$15-25/kW/month saved]
2. Energy arbitrage              [Buy low, use high; $20-40/kW/year]
3. Solar self-consumption boost  [Store excess solar for later use]
4. VPP participation             [Aggregated FCAS + wholesale]
5. Backup power                  [Avoided UPS/generator costs]
6. Network tariff optimisation   [Shift from demand to energy tariff]
```

## Demand Response Programs

### Available Programs

| Program | Description | C&I Participation |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Wholesale Demand Response Mechanism (WDRM) | NEM market-integrated demand response; dispatched by AEMO | Via Demand Response Service Provider (DRSP); min 1 MW aggregated |
| RERT | Emergency reserves contracted by AEMO | Large C&I with >10 MW interruptible load |
| DNSP demand management | Network-level peak reduction programs | Contracted directly with DNSP; 100 kW+ |
| Retailer peak demand programs | Incentive payments for reducing peak consumption | Via retail contract; varies by retailer |
| ARENA-funded DR trials | Innovation programs testing new DR models | Application-based; ARENA funding |

### Demand Response Economics

| Scenario | Revenue | Commitment |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| WDRM participation (aggregated) | $50-200/MWh for dispatched DR events | Availability and dispatch obligations |
| RERT contract | $500-5,000/MWh for emergency events | Rare activation; high per-event payment |
| DNSP peak reduction | $200-600/kW/year for guaranteed availability | 4-8 hour response windows; seasonal |
| Voluntary peak reduction (retailer) | $50-150/event | Low commitment; low payment |

## Emerging Trends

### EV Charging Infrastructure

- C&I sites becoming EV charging hubs (employee fleets, logistics depots)
- Solar + battery + EV charging co-optimisation
- Vehicle-to-grid (V2G) emerging for fleet vehicles
- Network tariff management critical for high-power DC charging

### Electrification of Industrial Processes

- Industrial heat pumps replacing gas boilers
- Electric arc furnaces, induction furnaces
- Solar thermal for low-grade process heat
- Hydrogen electrolysis at industrial sites

### Digital Energy Management

- AI-driven energy management systems optimising self-consumption, battery dispatch, demand management
- Real-time monitoring dashboards
- Predictive maintenance using IoT sensors
- Automated tariff switching and demand response participation
