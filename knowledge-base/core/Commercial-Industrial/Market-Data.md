# Market Data -- Commercial-Industrial (100 kW - 5 MW)

## C&I PPA Rates

### Current C&I PPA Market (2025-2026)

| PPA Type | Typical Rate (A$/MWh) | Tenor |
|----------|----------------------|-------|
| On-site solar PPA (rooftop) | $50-80 | 10-15 years |
| Behind-the-meter solar + battery PPA | $70-100 | 10-15 years |
| Off-site / virtual PPA (bundled with LGCs) | $55-90 | 7-15 years |
| Wholesale-referenced PPA | Spot + premium ($5-15 above pool) | 3-7 years |
| Fixed-price PPA | $60-90 | 7-15 years |

### PPA Pricing Factors

| Factor | Impact on Price |
|--------|----------------|
| System size | Larger systems achieve lower $/MWh |
| Location (solar resource) | QLD/SA lower than VIC/TAS |
| Roof condition / access | Complex roofs increase install cost |
| Counterparty credit | Investment-grade off-takers achieve better rates |
| Contract length | Longer tenor = lower rate |
| LGC bundling | Bundled LGCs add $3-5/MWh above energy-only |

## Network Tariffs

### Demand vs Energy Tariff Comparison

| Tariff Structure | Best For | Risk |
|-----------------|----------|------|
| Demand-based (kW) | Flat load profile; high energy use | Solar reduces energy but may not reduce peak demand |
| Energy-based (kWh) | Variable load; low demand peaks | Higher per-unit cost |
| Time-of-use (TOU) | Load flexible; can shift to off-peak | Peak rates during solar-poor hours |
| Critical peak pricing | Flexible load with DR capability | High penalty for peak events |

## Carbon Offsets

### Australian Carbon Credit Units (ACCUs)

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| ACCU spot price | ~$30-35 (2025-26) |
| Safeguard Mechanism | Facilities emitting >100 kt CO2-e must stay below declining baseline |
| C&I relevance | Large C&I users may need ACCUs for Scope 1 emissions compliance |
| Voluntary market | Corporate sustainability commitments driving voluntary ACCU demand |

### Voluntary Carbon Market

| Certificate Type | Price Range | Application |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------|
| ACCUs | $30-35/t CO2-e | Australian compliance and voluntary |
| LGCs | ~$3/MWh | Renewable energy attribute for corporate reporting |
| International offsets (VCUs, GS) | $5-30/t CO2-e | Scope 3 and voluntary commitments |
| RE100 / GreenPower | Included in retail contract premium | Retail-level renewable energy matching |

### Corporate Emission Reporting

| Framework | Relevance |
|-----------|-----------|
| NGER (National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting) | Mandatory for facilities >25 kt CO2-e |
| Climate Active certification | Voluntary; requires full carbon neutrality |
| TCFD / ISSB reporting | Increasingly required by ASX-listed companies |
| RE100 | Commitment to 100% renewable electricity |

## Economic Analysis: C&I Solar + Battery

### Typical Project Economics (500 kW Solar + 200 kW/400 kWh Battery)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| System cost (installed) | $550,000-$750,000 |
| Annual generation | ~800 MWh |
| Self-consumption rate | 65-80% |
| Annual electricity savings | $80,000-$120,000 |
| Annual demand charge savings (battery) | $15,000-$30,000 |
| LGC revenue | ~$2,400-3,200/year |
| Simple payback | 5-7 years |
| IRR (20-year) | 12-18% |
