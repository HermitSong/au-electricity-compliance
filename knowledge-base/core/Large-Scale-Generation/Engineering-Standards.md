# Engineering Standards -- Large-Scale Generation (>30 MW)

## AS/NZS Standards

### Electrical Wiring and Installation

| Standard | Title | Application |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| AS/NZS 3000:2018 | Wiring Rules | All electrical installations; mandatory for all grid-connected generation |
| AS/NZS 3008 | Electrical Installations -- Selection of Cables | Cable sizing for HV and LV systems |
| AS 2067 | Substations and High Voltage Installations | Substation design for utility-scale plants |
| AS/NZS 1768 | Lightning Protection | Lightning protection for exposed generation sites |

### Solar-Specific Standards

| Standard | Title | Application |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| AS/NZS 5033:2021 | Installation and Safety Requirements for Photovoltaic Arrays | PV array design, DC wiring, isolation, earthing |
| IEC 62548 (adopted) | Design Requirements for PV Arrays | Array layout, string sizing, protection |
| IEC 61730 | PV Module Safety | Module qualification and safety testing |
| IEC 61215 | PV Module Performance | Module performance qualification (crystalline Si) |
| IEC 62941 | PV Module Quality System | Manufacturing quality assurance |

### Wind-Specific Standards

| Standard | Title | Application |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| IEC 61400-1 | Wind Turbines -- Design Requirements | Turbine design class and loading |
| IEC 61400-3 | Design Requirements for Offshore Wind | Offshore foundation and turbine design |
| IEC 61400-12 | Power Performance Measurements | Turbine power curve verification |
| AS/NZS 1170 | Structural Design Actions | Wind loading on structures |

### Battery Storage Standards

| Standard | Title | Application |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| AS/NZS 5139:2019 | Electrical Installations -- Safety of BESS | Battery installation safety |
| IEC 62619 | Secondary Lithium Cells -- Safety | Cell-level safety requirements |
| IEC 63056 | Non-Industrial Use BESS Safety | Utility-scale safety (adopted) |
| NFPA 855 (referenced) | Standard for Installation of Stationary Energy Storage | Fire safety for BESS installations |

## IEC Standards Adopted in Australia

Australia broadly adopts IEC standards through Standards Australia with modifications published as AS/IEC or AS/NZS variants. Key adopted standards include:

- **IEC 61000 series** -- Electromagnetic compatibility (power quality, harmonics)
- **IEC 60076** -- Power transformers
- **IEC 62271** -- High-voltage switchgear and controlgear
- **IEC 61850** -- Communication networks and systems for power utility automation (SCADA/protection)
- **IEC 60909** -- Short-circuit currents in three-phase AC systems

## Clean Energy Council (CEC) Accreditation

### For Large-Scale Projects

- **CEC Accredited Designer** -- Required for designing solar PV systems installed under the RET
- **CEC Approved Equipment Lists** -- Modules and inverters must be listed on CEC approved product lists for eligibility under the LRET
- **CEC Guidelines for Large-Scale Solar** -- Best practice design, construction, and commissioning guidelines

### Accreditation Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Installer Accreditation | CEC-accredited installer must supervise installation for STC/LGC eligibility |
| Module Approval | Must be on CEC Approved Modules list |
| Inverter Approval | Must be on CEC Approved Inverters list |
| Design Review | Independent design review recommended for >5 MW |

## Construction and Commissioning

Large-scale projects must comply with:

- **AEMO Generator Registration** -- Registration as a Scheduled Generator or Semi-Scheduled Generator
- **AEMO Commissioning Requirements** -- R2 testing, GPS compliance testing per [[Grid-Requirements|NER S5.2.5]]
- **Network Service Provider (NSP) requirements** -- TNSP-specific connection standards
- **State planning approvals** -- Development consent under respective state planning legislation
