# Safety and Compliance -- Large-Scale Generation (>30 MW)

## Work Health and Safety (WHS)

### Federal Framework

The **Work Health and Safety Act 2011** (Cth) provides the model WHS legislation adopted (with variations) by most states and territories.

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Primary Duty of Care | Person conducting a business or undertaking (PCBU) must ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, the health and safety of workers |
| Risk Management | Must identify hazards, assess risks, implement controls (hierarchy of controls) |
| Incident Notification | Notifiable incidents must be reported to the state WHS regulator immediately |
| Safety Management System | Required for construction projects; recommended for all large-scale generation |

### State-Level OHS/WHS Regulators

| State | Regulator | Key Legislation |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| NSW | SafeWork NSW | Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (NSW) |
| VIC | WorkSafe Victoria | Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic) -- note: Victoria retains its own OHS Act |
| QLD | Workplace Health and Safety Queensland | Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (Qld) |
| SA | SafeWork SA | Work Health and Safety Act 2012 (SA) |
| TAS | WorkSafe Tasmania | Work Health and Safety Act 2012 (Tas) |
| WA | WorkSafe WA | Work Health and Safety Act 2020 (WA) |

### Electrical Safety

| State | Key Regulation |
|-------|---------------|
| NSW | Electricity Supply (Safety and Network Management) Regulation |
| VIC | Electricity Safety Act 1998; Electricity Safety (Installations) Regulations |
| QLD | Electrical Safety Act 2002; Electrical Safety Regulation 2013 |
| SA | Electricity Act 1996 |
| WA | Electricity Act 1945; Electricity Regulations 1947 |

Key electrical safety requirements for large-scale generation:
- Licensed electrical workers only
- High voltage switching procedures and permits
- Arc flash assessment and PPE requirements
- Isolation and lock-out/tag-out procedures
- DC safety protocols for solar PV (AS/NZS 5033)
- Battery storage fire safety plans (AS/NZS 5139)

## Environmental Compliance

### EPBC Act (Federal)

The **Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999** (Cth) applies to projects that may have a significant impact on Matters of National Environmental Significance (MNES).

| MNES Trigger | Relevance to Generation |
|-------------|------------------------|
| Threatened species and communities | Wind farms (bird/bat strike), solar (habitat clearing) |
| Wetlands of international importance | Projects near Ramsar wetlands |
| World Heritage properties | Proximity to listed areas |
| Commonwealth marine areas | Offshore wind projects |
| Water resources (coal seam gas/mining) | Gas generation projects |

**Process:**
1. Referral to Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEEW)
2. Assessment of whether action is a "controlled action"
3. If controlled: Environmental Impact Assessment (EIS) required
4. Ministerial approval with conditions

### State Environmental Legislation

| State | Key Act | Application |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| NSW | Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 | State Significant Development (SSD) for >30 MW |
| VIC | Environment Effects Act 1978 | Environment Effects Statement (EES) may be required |
| QLD | Environmental Protection Act 1994 | Environmental Authority required |
| SA | Environment Protection Act 1993 | EPA licence for generation activities |
| TAS | Environmental Management and Pollution Control Act 1994 | Environmental assessment |
| WA | Environmental Protection Act 1986 | EPA referral and assessment |

## Development Approval Process

### Typical Pathway for Large-Scale Projects

```
1. Site Selection & Feasibility
   ├── Environmental desktop study
   ├── Grid connection inquiry to TNSP/AEMO
   └── Community engagement plan

2. Planning Approval
   ├── State planning application (SSD in NSW, Planning Permit in VIC)
   ├── EPBC referral (if MNES triggers)
   ├── Visual impact assessment
   ├── Noise assessment
   ├── Traffic management plan
   ├── Aboriginal heritage assessment
   └── Agricultural land use assessment

3. Grid Connection
   ├── AEMO connection enquiry
   ├── Connection application to TNSP
   ├── System strength assessment
   ├── Generator Performance Standards negotiation
   └── Connection agreement execution

4. Construction
   ├── WHS management plan
   ├── Environmental management plan
   ├── Construction Environmental Management Plan (CEMP)
   └── Hold points and inspections

5. Commissioning
   ├── AEMO R2 testing
   ├── GPS compliance verification
   ├── Grid protection settings verification
   └── Registration as Scheduled/Semi-Scheduled Generator
```

### Timeframes (Indicative)

| Phase | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Feasibility and planning application | 12-24 months |
| Planning assessment and approval | 6-18 months |
| EPBC assessment (if required) | 6-12 months |
| Grid connection process | 12-36 months |
| Construction | 12-24 months |
| Commissioning | 3-6 months |
| **Total (typical)** | **3-7 years** |

## Ongoing Compliance

| Requirement | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| AEMO registration renewal | As required |
| Generator Performance Standards compliance | Continuous; periodic testing |
| NGER reporting (if >25 kt CO2-e) | Annual |
| Environmental monitoring (conditions of consent) | As per approval conditions |
| WHS audits | Annual or as required |
| Electrical safety inspections | As per state regulation |
