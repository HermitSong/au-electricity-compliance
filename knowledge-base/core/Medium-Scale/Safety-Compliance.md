# Safety and Compliance -- Medium-Scale Generation (5-30 MW)

## Work Health and Safety

The same WHS framework applies as for [[../Large-Scale-Generation/Safety-Compliance|large-scale generation]]. Key requirements:

| Requirement | Application to Medium-Scale |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| WHS Act compliance | Mandatory; PCBU duties apply |
| Risk assessment | Required before construction and operation |
| Electrical safety | Licensed electrical workers; isolation procedures |
| DC safety (solar) | AS/NZS 5033 compliance; DC isolator requirements |
| Battery safety (BESS) | AS/NZS 5139; fire safety management plan |
| Working at heights | For solar and wind maintenance |
| Incident notification | Notifiable incidents reported to state WHS regulator |

## State Planning Approval

Medium-scale projects (5-30 MW) typically fall below the threshold for State Significant Development but still require development approval.

### Planning Pathways by State

| State | Pathway | Threshold |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| NSW | Development Application (DA) to local council or Regional Planning Panel | SSD for >30 MW; DA for 5-30 MW |
| VIC | Planning Permit from local council | Call-in by Minister for >10 MW possible |
| QLD | Development Application under Planning Act 2016 | Material Change of Use; Operational Works |
| SA | Development Application under Planning, Development and Infrastructure Act 2016 | Impact Assessed or Code Assessed depending on zone |
| TAS | Development Application under Land Use Planning and Approvals Act 1993 | Discretionary use in most zones |
| WA | Development Application under Planning and Development Act 2005 | Local government approval |

### Typical Planning Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Visual Impact Assessment | Photomontages from key viewpoints; glare analysis for solar |
| Noise Assessment | Compliance with relevant state noise guidelines (e.g., NSW Noise Policy for Industry) |
| Traffic Management Plan | Construction traffic routes, road upgrades |
| Biodiversity Assessment | Flora and fauna survey; offset requirements |
| Aboriginal Heritage | Aboriginal Heritage Information Management System (AHIMS) search in NSW; equivalent in other states |
| Agricultural Impact | Assessment of impact on agricultural land use |
| Bushfire Risk | Asset Protection Zones; Emergency Management Plan |
| Decommissioning Plan | Plan for end-of-life removal and site rehabilitation |

## Environmental Assessment

### EPBC Act Referral

Projects 5-30 MW may still trigger the EPBC Act if they impact Matters of National Environmental Significance. A self-assessment against MNES triggers is recommended early in project development.

| Trigger | Common for Medium-Scale |
|---------|----------------------|
| Threatened species | Yes -- site-specific surveys required |
| Wetlands (Ramsar) | If within proximity |
| Heritage areas | If within proximity |

### State Environmental Requirements

| State | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| NSW | Review of Environmental Factors (REF) or Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) |
| VIC | Referral to DEECA if significant environmental effects likely |
| QLD | Environmental Authority under Environmental Protection Act 1994 |
| SA | Referral to EPA SA for potential environmental impacts |

## Ongoing Compliance

| Requirement | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| Planning consent conditions | Continuous; annual reporting if required |
| NGER reporting | If facility emits >25 kt CO2-e (unlikely for renewables) |
| Environmental monitoring | As per consent conditions (noise, ecology) |
| WHS management | Continuous; periodic audits |
| Electrical safety inspection | As per state electrical safety regulations |
| DNSP compliance | Annual connection compliance review |
