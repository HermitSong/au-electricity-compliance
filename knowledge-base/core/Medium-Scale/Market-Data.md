# Market Data -- Medium-Scale Generation (5-30 MW)

## Behind-the-Meter Economics

Many medium-scale systems are installed behind the meter at industrial or commercial sites, avoiding network charges on self-consumed electricity.

### Value Stack for Behind-the-Meter Medium-Scale

| Revenue/Savings Component | Typical Value (A$/MWh) |
|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Avoided retail electricity cost | $80-150 |
| Avoided network charges (demand) | $50-120/kW/year |
| Avoided network charges (energy) | $20-60 |
| LGC revenue | ~$3-4 (current spot) |
| Export revenue (excess generation) | $40-80 (wholesale or PPA) |
| FCAS revenue (if battery included) | $5-20 |

### Net Value of Behind-the-Meter Solar (Indicative)

| Scenario | Self-Consumption Rate | Effective Value |
|----------|----------------------|----------------|
| High self-consumption (industrial load) | 70-90% | $90-140/MWh |
| Moderate self-consumption | 40-70% | $60-100/MWh |
| Low self-consumption (weekday only) | 20-40% | $40-70/MWh |

Behind-the-meter value heavily depends on the load profile match with solar generation.

## Merchant vs Contracted Revenue

### Merchant Operation

Medium-scale generators selling into the NEM as merchant:

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Revenue basis | NEM spot price at regional reference node |
| Volume risk | Full exposure to weather/output variability |
| Price risk | Full exposure to spot price volatility |
| Average merchant revenue (solar, 2025) | $40-70/MWh (region-dependent; declining daytime prices) |
| Average merchant revenue (wind, 2025) | $50-90/MWh |

### Contracted (PPA)

| Contract Type | Typical Terms |
|--------------|---------------|
| Corporate PPA | 7-15 years; fixed or indexed price; $35-60/MWh |
| Retail PPA | 5-10 years; bundled with retail supply |
| Government PPA | CIS-underwritten; revenue floor guarantee |
| Hybrid | Mix of contracted floor + merchant upside |

### Merchant vs Contracted Comparison

| Aspect | Merchant | Contracted (PPA) |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| Revenue certainty | Low | High |
| Upside potential | High (volatile) | Capped |
| Bankability | Difficult to finance | Preferred by lenders |
| Suitable for | Batteries, flexible assets | Solar, wind |
| LGC treatment | Sold separately on spot | Often bundled in PPA price |

## Mini-Grid Opportunities

Remote and fringe-of-grid areas in Australia present opportunities for medium-scale mini-grids.

### Target Segments

| Segment | Typical Size | Location |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Mining operations | 5-30 MW | Remote WA, QLD, NT |
| Agricultural districts | 5-15 MW | Regional NSW, VIC, QLD |
| Island communities | 1-10 MW | Torres Strait, Kangaroo Island |
| Aboriginal communities | 0.5-5 MW | Remote NT, WA, SA |
| Fringe-of-grid townships | 5-20 MW | Western NSW, SA, WA |

### Mini-Grid Economics

| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Avoided fuel cost | $150-400/MWh (diesel generation) |
| Renewable LCOE | $40-80/MWh (solar + battery) |
| Net savings | $70-320/MWh over diesel |
| Funding support | [[../Regulatory-Bodies#ARENA|ARENA]] grants; CEFC concessional finance; state programs |

### Regulatory Framework for Mini-Grids

- Stand-alone power systems (SAPS) framework under NER amendments
- DNSP obligation to consider SAPS as alternative to network extension
- Regulatory sandbox provisions for innovative mini-grid models

## Revenue Comparison: Medium-Scale Project Types

| Project Type | Typical Revenue (A$/MWh) | Revenue Certainty |
|-------------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| Behind-the-meter solar (high self-consumption) | $90-140 | High |
| PPA-contracted solar farm | $35-60 | High |
| Merchant solar farm | $40-70 | Low |
| Behind-the-meter battery (C&I) | $80-200 (arbitrage + demand charges) | Medium |
| Merchant battery (arbitrage + FCAS) | $100-250 | Medium-Low |
| Remote mini-grid (solar + battery) | $150-300 (vs diesel avoided cost) | High |
