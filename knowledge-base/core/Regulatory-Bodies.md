# Australian Energy Regulatory Bodies

## Jurisdiction Boundary

| Framework | Geographic and legal scope | Principal bodies | Critical distinction |
|-----------|----------------------------|------------------|----------------------|
| **NEM wholesale and power system** | QLD, NSW, VIC, SA and TAS; ACT participates through the NSW region | AEMO, AEMC and AER | Victoria is in the NEM even though it is outside the NECF retail framework |
| **NECF retail** | NSW, QLD, SA, TAS and ACT under jurisdictional application legislation | AER and AEMC, with retained state or territory functions | Does not apply in Victoria, WA or NT |
| **Victorian retail** | Victoria | ESC under Victorian legislation and the Energy Retail Code of Practice | Separate from AER retailer authorisation and NERR retail compliance |
| **WEM / SWIS** | South West Interconnected System in WA | AEMO as operator, WA Coordinator of Energy and ERA under WA instruments | Separate wholesale, capacity and prudential framework; not NEM or NECF |
| **Northern Territory** | NT electricity systems under Territory legislation | Utilities Commission and Power and Water, with other NT bodies as applicable | Separate from NEM, NECF and WEM; verify current NT legislation and licence conditions |

## Federal Bodies

### AEMO

**Australian Energy Market Operator**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Role | Market and grid operator |
| Jurisdiction | NEM (eastern states + SA, TAS), gas markets, WEM (WA) |
| Website | [aemo.com.au](https://www.aemo.com.au) |

Key responsibilities:
- Operates the NEM dispatch engine (5-minute intervals)
- Manages power system security and reliability
- Publishes the [[Market-Structure/NEM-Overview|Integrated System Plan (ISP)]]
- Manages generator connections and performance standards
- Operates the WEM in Western Australia
- **Operates NEM settlement & prudential** — sets each participant's Maximum Credit Limit (MCL); ran the 9 Aug 2026 SSC go-live → see [[Market-Structure/Settlement-Prudential|Settlement & Prudential (SSC / MCL)]]
- Publishes demand forecasts, generation information, and [[Large-Scale-Generation/Market-Data|market data]]

### AER

**Australian Energy Regulator**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Role | Economic regulator |
| Jurisdiction | NER wholesale and economic regulation in NEM jurisdictions; NECF retail only in participating jurisdictions; not the WA or NT market regulator |
| Website | [aer.gov.au](https://www.aer.gov.au) |

Key responsibilities:
- Sets and enforces network revenue allowances for TNSPs and DNSPs
- Monitors wholesale and retail market performance
- Enforces the National Electricity Rules in NEM jurisdictions and the National Energy Retail Law and Rules in NECF jurisdictions; Victorian retail enforcement sits with the ESC → see [[Market-Structure/Retail-Compliance|Retail Compliance (NERL/NERR)]]
- Publishes the annual State of the Energy Market report
- Sets the [[Market-Structure/Retail-Pricing-DMO-VDO|Default Market Offer (DMO)]] (retail price safety net) for NSW, SA, SE QLD
- Regulates network revenue (DUoS/TUoS) and Tariff Structure Statements → see [[Market-Structure/Network-Pricing-and-Metering|Network Pricing & Metering]]
- Monitors generator compliance with performance standards

### CER

**Clean Energy Regulator**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Role | Administers renewable energy and emissions reduction schemes |
| Jurisdiction | National |
| Website | [cer.gov.au](https://www.cer.gov.au) |

Key responsibilities:
- Administers the Renewable Energy Target (RET): Large-scale (LRET) and Small-scale (SRES)
- Issues [[Large-Scale-Generation/Market-Data|LGCs (Large-scale Generation Certificates)]] and [[Residential/Market-Data|STCs (Small-scale Technology Certificates)]]
- Administers the Safeguard Mechanism (emissions caps for large facilities)
- Manages the Australian Carbon Credit Unit (ACCU) scheme
- Operates the National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting (NGER) scheme

### AEMC

**Australian Energy Market Commission**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Role | Rule maker and market development advisor |
| Jurisdiction | National electricity and energy retail rule-making framework as adopted and applied by participating jurisdictions; WA and NT require separate treatment |
| Website | [aemc.gov.au](https://www.aemc.gov.au) |

Key responsibilities:
- Makes and amends the National Electricity Rules (**NER — current consolidated v251, 23 Jul 2026**) and the National Energy Retail Rules
- Made the **Shortening the Settlement Cycle (SSC) rule, ERC0384** (live 9 Aug 2026) → see [[Market-Structure/Settlement-Prudential]]
- Conducts market reviews (e.g., reliability standards, price caps)
- Sets the Market Price Cap (**$20,300/MWh FY2025-26**) and Cumulative Price Threshold annually
- Advises on energy market reform

### ARENA

**Australian Renewable Energy Agency**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Role | Funding body for renewable energy innovation |
| Jurisdiction | National |
| Website | [arena.gov.au](https://www.arena.gov.au) |

Key responsibilities:
- Provides grants and funding for early-stage renewable energy projects
- Funds large-scale solar, battery storage, hydrogen, and demand response projects
- Knowledge-sharing platform for project outcomes
- Supports VPP trials, grid integration research, and offshore wind studies

### CEFC

**Clean Energy Finance Corporation**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Role | Green bank / concessional finance |
| Jurisdiction | National |
| Website | [cefc.com.au](https://www.cefc.com.au) |

Key responsibilities:
- Provides debt and equity finance for clean energy projects
- Finances large-scale renewables, storage, grid infrastructure
- Supports C&I energy efficiency and behind-the-meter projects
- Manages the Rewiring the Nation fund (transmission investment)
- Operates the Household Energy Upgrades Fund

## State-Level Bodies

### New South Wales

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| NSW Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEEW) | Energy policy, planning approvals |
| EnergyCo NSW | REZ delivery and coordination |

### Victoria

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA) | Energy policy |
| VicGrid | REZ planning and delivery |
| Essential Services Commission (ESC) | Victorian retail licensing, code enforcement, price regulation and [[Residential/Market-Data\|feed-in tariff]] setting |

### Queensland

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Queensland Department of Energy and Climate | Energy policy, REZ coordination |
| Queensland Energy System Advisory Board | Strategic advice |
| Powerlink Queensland | Transmission network service provider |

### South Australia

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Department for Energy and Mining | Energy policy |
| ESCOSA (Essential Services Commission of SA) | Retained state licensing and economic functions; AER administers NECF retail compliance |
| ElectraNet | Transmission network service provider |

### Tasmania

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Department of State Growth | Energy policy |
| TasNetworks | Transmission and distribution |
| Office of the Tasmanian Economic Regulator | Retained Tasmanian economic and price functions; AER administers NECF retail compliance |

### Western Australia

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Department of Energy, Mines, Industry Regulation and Safety (DEMIRS) | Energy policy |
| Economic Regulation Authority (ERA) | Market regulation for WEM |
| Western Power | Distribution and transmission in SWIS |

### Australian Capital Territory

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| ACT Government | Energy policy and jurisdictional legislation |
| Independent Competition and Regulatory Commission (ICRC) | Retained retail price regulation; AER administers NECF retail compliance |
| Evoenergy | Electricity distribution network service provider |

### Northern Territory

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Utilities Commission | Territory electricity licensing and economic regulation under the NT framework |
| Power and Water | Network and system functions identified in the NT connection material covered by this knowledge base |
