# D00 - Australian Electricity Enforcement Event Timeline, 2006-2026

This page is a selected official-source index of Australian electricity enforcement records and major official technical events from 2006 to 2026. It is not an exhaustive register. Low-value infringement notices, state safety prosecutions and network technical incidents must be checked against the relevant regulator's current register before litigation-grade use. For Australia-wide source-series coverage, read D17 and `data/enforcement-source-register.json`.

Load-bearing claims in this page are tied to official regulator or court sources. A media report may be retained only as a `media-trigger`; it is not a factual basis for stating that a contravention occurred.

## Status labels

| label | meaning |
|---|---|
| `current` | Usable as a current enforcement reference, subject to checking current rule text and penalty indexation. |
| `old-rule` | Decided under an earlier rule or penalty regime. Use for history and control design, not as direct current law. |
| `quashed-on-appeal` | Earlier judgment or penalty outcome has been set aside on appeal. Use the later appellate position. |
| `proceedings` | Proceedings have commenced, but allegations have not been finally determined. |
| `technical-event` | Official technical investigation or system event, not by itself a finding of contravention. |
| `media-trigger` | Media-reported customer or operational event retained as a risk signal only; not an official finding or precedent. |
| `review-guidance` | Regulator review, decision letter, guideline or warning rather than a penalty. |
| `reported-breach` | A licensee-reported or regulator-identified non-compliance without a court penalty or infringement notice. |
| `administrative-resolution` | A matter resolved through correction, reimbursement, write-off, warning or monitoring rather than punitive action. |
| `licence-action` | Licence suspension, cancellation, variation or enforceable licence condition. |
| `ombudsman-order` | Binding dispute-resolution order; not a regulator penalty or court judgment. |
| `public-warning` | Named safety warning; not by itself a prosecution, conviction or admission. |

## Western Australia / ERA and WEM distinctions

Western Australia is not part of the NEM or the NECF. The ERA is Western Australia's economic regulator and monitors compliance with the Wholesale Electricity Market and electricity retail licensing frameworks. WEM records must not be converted into NER/NERR conclusions without a separate legal bridge.

## Coverage caveats

- This page is a selected chronology, not a complete enforcement register. D17 and `data/enforcement-source-register.json` identify the national, state, territory, safety and ombudsman series that must be checked for a completeness claim.
- State and territory entries preserve local licensing, code, safety, complaint and system-control outcomes that do not appear in AER registers. WA and NT sit outside the NEM and NECF retail framework; Victoria is in the NEM but outside the NECF.
- A `reported-breach`, `administrative-resolution`, `ombudsman-order`, `public-warning` or `technical-event` must not be restated as a court finding or civil penalty.
- Better Bills is represented by AER review and decision-letter records, not by a named large AER penalty case in this KB.
- Centrepay entries must be rechecked after any further Origin, Alinta, AER or AEMC action following the 2026 AGL Full Court decision.
