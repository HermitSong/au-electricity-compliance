# Contributing

Accuracy and useful evidence take priority over document or question counts.
Contribute in English. Preserve official names, defined terms, dates and status.

## Corrections

1. Identify the public file and claim, its jurisdiction, relevant actor/activity
   and the conduct or proposed-action date.
2. Supply an official source URL, exact clause or judgment pinpoint, applicable
   version and commencement information. Distinguish allegations from findings,
   final orders from appeal treatment, and historical rules from current ones.
3. Explain the correction and any unresolved exception. Do not infer authority
   from publication recency or silently promote candidate mappings.
4. Add focused regression tests when behaviour changes. Do not rewrite frozen
   diagnostic answers or scores after seeing the gold; use a new development run.
5. Rebuild the local index, run the portable release tests described in
   `EVALUATION.md`, and inspect the actual staged diff before submitting.

Do not broaden a clause approval just because a whole instrument was downloaded.
Source text stays in the separately reviewed private archive, with provenance,
extraction checks and independent scoped review. Missing citations and substantively false answers
must be reported as separate defects.

## Public Data Boundary

Submit source links, identifiers and independently authored factual summaries,
not PDFs, screenshots, page copies or OCR/full-text exports. This policy applies
even where a particular publisher permits redistribution. Use the local
acquisition Skill for private source collection. Run both working-tree and staged
release scans before pushing; CI runs after upload and cannot prevent disclosure
in already-public commits, issues or PR attachments. See `DISTRIBUTION.md`.

Never post customer identifiers, account details, credentials, real meetings,
private company context, privileged advice, gold sets or private output bundles
in commits, issues, PRs or support attachments. Use purpose-written synthetic
examples, not lightly renamed real records. See `SECURITY.md`.

## Contribution Licence

Submit only material you have the right to contribute. Original contributions
must use `LICENSE`, subject to `LICENSING.md`. Retain third-party attribution.
You retain ownership. For separate commercial licensing, a PR containing original
code or authored content must expressly acknowledge this additional grant:

> I have authority to contribute the identified original material. I license my
> contribution under the project Source-Available Licence 1.0 and additionally
> grant HermitSong, as this project's maintainer, a perpetual, worldwide,
> non-exclusive, royalty-free copyright licence to reproduce, modify, distribute
> and sublicense my original contribution as part of this project, including
> under separate commercial terms. I retain ownership. This grant excludes
> third-party material and does not affect mandatory statutory rights.

Do not infer this grant from a fork, an issue, silence or an earlier contribution.
Do not merge original code/content without recording express acknowledgement or
a separate rights agreement. Source-link corrections and public facts do not
become copyrighted contributions merely because they were submitted.

Employer-owned work needs employer authority. Identify separately licensed
material. This process cannot grant rights over other people's work, official
documents or private company records. It is a review procedure, not an automated
legal-consent check or proof that the contributor owns the submitted material.
